Universidad Construrama, a retail network trained on one platform

Universidad Construrama is the online training platform of the Construrama retail network, a chain of building materials stores in Mexico. Students register there, take their courses and earn their certificates. I built it in 2018 and have maintained and run it ever since.

AThe starting point

  • A network of retailers spread across the country.
  • Staff at every store who need the same training.
  • Courses, terms and certificates to keep track of, student by student.

BToday

  • Students register and enroll online.
  • Courses are taken from anywhere, by term.
  • Certificates are issued from the same platform.
  • The program team validates students, stores and retailers.
