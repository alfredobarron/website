What makes a website work

A business website has one job: that whoever is looking for you finds you, understands what you do and gets in touch. For that I take care of four things:

  • That it loads in a couple of seconds, on mobile data too.
  • That the first screen says what you do, for whom and where.
  • That Google and AI assistants can read it and cite it.
  • That contacting you takes a single tap.

Frequently asked questions

Do you use templates or WordPress?

No. Each site is designed and coded for the business. That is why it loads faster, does not depend on plugins that need updating and is harder to break into.

Can I change the text and photos later?

Yes. Minor changes are included in the maintenance plan, or I leave the site ready for your team to make them.

Will the site show up on Google?

The site is delivered optimized for search engines and registered in Google Search Console. The ranking also depends on the competition and on time: search positioning is ongoing work.
