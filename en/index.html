How I work, from A to B

You talk to the person who designs and builds your software, from start to finish.

  1. Call

    You tell me how your business works today and where you want to get. It is free.

  2. Proposal

    You get the scope in writing, with a fixed price per stage and delivery dates.

  3. Build

    I work in stages, and at each one you test something that already works.

  4. Launch

    I leave the system on your servers, the apps in the stores and your team trained.

  5. Operation

    I stay in charge of maintenance, monitoring and the improvements your business asks for.

Frequently asked questions

How much does custom software or an app cost?

It depends on the scope: how many screens, how many types of user and which other systems it connects to. After a free call I send you a quote with a fixed price per stage, so you know the cost and the dates before we start.

How long does a project take?

A website usually takes a few weeks. A custom system or a mobile app takes two to four months for a first version your team can already use; after that it keeps improving in stages.

Do you work with companies outside Mexico?

Yes. I work remotely with companies across Mexico and the United States, in English or Spanish, and in person in the Monterrey area when the project calls for it.

Do you publish the app on the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. I handle the developer accounts, the store listing, the privacy policy, the Apple and Google reviews and the updates that follow.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. I offer monthly maintenance plans that cover hosting, monitoring, bug fixes and the new features your business asks for.

What technologies do you use?

Laravel (PHP) on the server, Vue for web dashboards, React Native with Expo for iOS and Android apps, and Amazon Web Services for infrastructure. I also use Python with FastAPI for APIs and services, and AI assists me through the whole development process. They all have large communities, so any other team can continue the project.
