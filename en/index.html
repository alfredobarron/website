FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does custom software or an app cost?

It depends on scope. After a free call I send you a fixed quote by stages, so you know the cost and timeline before we start. A website usually takes a few weeks; a custom platform or mobile app, two to four months for the first version.

Do you work with companies outside Mexico?

Yes. I work remotely with companies in Mexico and the US, in English or Spanish. Monterrey shares the Central time zone, so we can collaborate in real time during your business hours.

Can you publish my app on the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. I handle the whole process: store listing, privacy policy, review requirements and updates. I build iOS and Android apps from a single React Native codebase, which lowers the cost of building and maintaining both.

Who owns the code?

You do. The source code, servers and accounts are in your name, with documentation, so you are never locked in.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. I offer monthly maintenance plans that cover hosting, monitoring, bug fixes and new features as your business grows.
